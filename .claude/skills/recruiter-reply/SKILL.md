# Recruiter replies

## His templates

Live on the Notion Carrier page itself (`15125c54-2a47-80a0-9ffd-cd9ad6c0b2c0`),
under "Resposta padrão recruiters". A copy is in `references/templates.md`. **Fetch the
Notion page** before replying — he edits these.

Five variants: standard first reply · first reply with a question · visa sponsorship
question · follow-up · remote-only pushback.

## Filling them in

Each template has `xxx` for the recruiter's name and a gap after "aligns perfectly
with my background in 😊" — fill that with the specific overlap between *their* job
description and his actual experience. Not "data engineering" — something like
"building agentic analytics tooling on top of large-scale Databricks pipelines".
That blank is the whole point of the template; don't leave it generic.

## His constraints

- **Fully remote only.** If the posting is hybrid or onsite, use the remote-only
  pushback template *before* investing in the process — he wrote it precisely to avoid
  wasted rounds. Ask about flexibility, don't refuse outright.
- **Ask about visa sponsorship** when the role is outside Brazil.
- He's Brazil-based, UTC-3 — worth mentioning for roles with heavy timezone overlap
  requirements.

## Tone

Warm, short, slightly informal. He uses "This opportunity is awesome!" and an emoji.
Keep it — it's his register, not a mistake to correct. Don't formalize him into a
cover letter.

## If the message came through Gmail

Gmail is connected. You can read the thread for context. **Draft only — never send.**
Put it in a draft or show it to him; the send is his call.
